# Repository contents

- `Paper-01-Mobility-Aware-Task-Offloading/` — Transformer-based vehicle mobility prediction and MAPPO-based offloading in UAV-MEC-assisted IoV.
- `Paper-02-Reliability-Aware-Task-Offloading/` — Task-driven reliability-aware offloading using dependence-aware latent action representation and TD3.
- `Paper-03-Trust-Enforcing-Pricing-Sensor-Cloud/` — DETER: bi-level trust calculation and Stackelberg-game dynamic pricing for Sensors-as-a-Service in sensor-cloud.
- `cross-paper-comparison.md` — Comparison of the three papers, their distinct research settings, and possible synthesis questions.

Each paper directory contains a README, concept notes, methodology, technical deep dive, experiments/results, limitations/insights, interview preparation, learning journal, and references.
Hope you enjoy reading.
