# Research Reading Portfolio

This repository collects structured technical reading notes for selected research papers. Each paper is documented as an individual case study, covering the research problem, key concepts, methodology, algorithms, experiments, critical observations, and interview preparation.

The aim is to make research reading both professionally presentable and useful for long-term learning.

## How each paper is documented

Each paper directory contains:

- A concise paper overview.
- Plain-language concept notes.
- A methodology and architecture walkthrough.
- A technical deep dive into important equations and algorithms.
- Experimental setup and results.
- Critical observations, assumptions, and open research questions.
- Interview preparation and a personal learning journal.
- Reference information and attribution.

## Cross-paper learning

Papers 01 and 02 both study UAV-assisted mobile edge computing, but focus on different bottlenecks: mobility-aware decision-making and reliability-aware task success. Paper 03 moves to a different but related network-services setting: a multi-owner sensor-cloud where trust, multi-hop routing, and economic incentives influence service quality. The three papers are not one combined algorithm; they offer complementary perspectives on how distributed network resources can be selected, coordinated, and governed under constraints.

See [`cross-paper-comparison.md`](cross-paper-comparison.md) for a structured synthesis.

## Scope and integrity

This is a collection of independent reading notes and technical analyses of published work. It does not claim authorship of the papers or reproduction of their experiments unless explicitly documented and independently completed. Original publications remain the work of their respective authors; links and citations are provided in each paper folder.
