## Selected background references cited by the paper

1. A. Jøsang and R. Ismail, “The Beta Reputation System,” in *Proceedings of the 15th Bled Electronic Commerce Conference*, vol. 5, 2002, pp. 2502–2511. This is the reputation-model background used for belief, disbelief, and uncertainty.
2. S. Misra, S. Chatterjee, and M. S. Obaidat, “On Theoretical Modeling of Sensor Cloud: A Paradigm Shift From Wireless Sensor Network,” *IEEE Systems Journal*, vol. 11, no. 2, pp. 1084–1093, June 2017. This is one of the sensor-cloud architecture references.
3. S. Chatterjee, R. Ladia, and S. Misra, “Dynamic Optimal Pricing for Heterogeneous Service-Oriented Architecture of Sensor-Cloud Infrastructure,” *IEEE Transactions on Services Computing*, vol. 10, no. 2, pp. 203–216, March 2017. This is the DOP pricing baseline/background.
4. K. Ahmed and M. Gregory, “Integrating Wireless Sensor Networks with Cloud Computing,” in *Proceedings of the 7th International Conference on Mobile Ad-hoc and Sensor Networks*, December 2011, pp. 364–366.
5. S. Chatterjee and S. Misra, “Optimal Composition of a Virtual Sensor for Efficient Virtualization within Sensor-Cloud,” in *Proceedings of IEEE International Conference on Communications*, June 2015, pp. 448–453.
